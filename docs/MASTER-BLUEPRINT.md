
# TAEX — THE ADEDAMOLA EXPRESS
## MASTER PLATFORM BLUEPRINT

Version: 1.0

## 1. BRAND IDENTITY

TAEX stands for The Adedamola Express.

- T = The
- A = Adedamola
- EX = Express

TAEX is a multi-service logistics, mobility, travel and executive support company.

Brand colours:
- Navy Blue: #0B3D91
- Orange: #FF6B00
- White: #FFFFFF

The platform must have a premium, modern, professional and mobile-friendly design.

## 2. TAEX SERVICE DIVISIONS

The platform must support five independent service divisions.

### Division 1: TAEX Logistics
- Parcel delivery
- Document delivery
- Same-day delivery
- Business and corporate deliveries
- Pickup and delivery across approved service locations

### Division 2: TAEX Executive Mobility
- Private transportation
- Chauffeur services
- Airport pickups and drop-offs
- Executive transportation
- VIP transportation

### Division 3: TAEX Personal & VIP Escort
- Personal travel accompaniment
- VIP assistance
- Executive travel support
- Personal journey assistance

### Division 4: TAEX Security Escort
- Security escort arrangements
- Executive movement support
- Journey security coordination

All security services must use appropriately qualified, insured and legally authorised providers.

### Division 5: TAEX Travel & Transfers
- Inter-state travel
- Inter-city transfers
- Travel booking coordination
- International travel coordination through suitable partners where available.

These divisions are planned services. The website must not advertise a service as operational until TAEX has confirmed its capacity, providers and legal requirements.

## 3. ONE PLATFORM, FIVE DIVISIONS

TAEX must have one central website and platform.

Customers should be able to:
- Create one account.
- Log in once.
- View all available TAEX services.
- Book different services.
- View their booking history.
- Track booking progress.
- Receive notifications.
- Contact customer support.

Each division must have its own booking form, pricing system, availability rules and operational workflow.

Do not use logistics pricing rules for transportation, travel or escort services.

## 6. LOGISTICS PRICING

The initial operating base is Ikorodu, Lagos.

The intended coverage is across Lagos, with additional routes activated only when operational costs and availability are verified.

The agreed logistics pricing formula is:

CUSTOMER PRICE = VERIFIED RIDER COST + ₦1,500 TAEX PROFIT.

This formula applies to logistics only.

The system must:
- Calculate prices on the server.
- Use verified rider costs.
- Prevent customers from seeing internal rider costs.
- Prevent bookings on routes without approved pricing.
- Allow authorised administrators to update pricing.
- Support standard and express delivery options when available.

Do not invent rider costs or route prices.

## 7. PAYMENT SYSTEM

Initial payment method: bank transfer.

The booking process should work as follows:

1. Customer creates a booking.
2. System calculates the price.
3. Customer receives the correct bank payment instructions.
4. Customer transfers the money.
5. Customer clicks "I've Made Payment".
6. Booking changes to "Verification Pending".
7. Administrator checks the actual bank account.
8. Administrator confirms or rejects the payment.
9. Customer receives an update.

A customer clicking the payment button must never automatically confirm payment.

Do not invent bank details or expose private banking information.

## 8. BOOKING EXPIRY

The current planning rule is:

Unpaid bookings expire after 60 minutes.

Expired bookings must be clearly marked.

Late transfers must be handled through an authorised administrative reconciliation process.

## 9. RIDER AND DRIVER MANAGEMENT

The platform should support:
- Rider registration.
- Driver/provider registration.
- Admin approval.
- Assignment to bookings.
- Availability management.
- Booking status updates.
- Completed journey records.
- Performance reports.

Each service division must use appropriate resources.

A parcel delivery rider must not automatically be treated as an executive driver or security escort.

## 10. SECURITY AND DATA PROTECTION

The platform must include:
- Secure authentication.
- Role-based permissions.
- Database access controls.
- Server-side validation.
- Protected administrator accounts.
- Secure handling of customer information.
- Audit logs.
- Backups.
- Error handling.

Passwords, API keys, bank credentials and private customer data must never be placed in public source code.

## 11. DEVELOPMENT ARCHITECTURE

Build a modular application with:

- Shared customer accounts.

- Shared payment records.
- Shared reporting.

- Separate booking workflows.

- Separate availability management.

The platform must be tested before public launch.
